## Checks
- **Hosts contacted by the tab:** only `markwhiting.github.io` and `api.github.com`. **Token in any request body:** none.
- **Speed:** steps advanced at 2.0 per second throughout, including while the page was not focused (headless).
- **Spike 1's answer:** a page can open PRs to its own repo using only in-page JS and a token in localStorage. A classic token with `repo` or `public_repo` scope works. Fine-grained tokens and the **fork path** (contributors without push access) are still untested live.

## Found and fixed during go-live
- **CI-only failure:** a hard-coded screenshot path. Fixed, and a guard test added.
- **Misleading status:** the page said "All session logs are shared" while a log was waiting between timed sends. It now reports "N session logs waiting…".
